With embeds on (`supports.embeds` in `config/app.php`), a YouTube or Vimeo link on its own line becomes a video. By default it's "click to load": a play button, with the thumbnail for YouTube, that loads the real player when clicked. Without the theme's script, the play button is a link to the video.
