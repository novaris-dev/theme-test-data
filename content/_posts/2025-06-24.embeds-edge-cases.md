Only a link on its own line is embedded. Everything here should stay a normal link.

A video link in a sentence stays a link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ.

[A video link with its own text](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ) stays a link.

A link on its own line that isn't a video:

https://example.com/a-page

A YouTube channel, not a video:

https://www.youtube.com/@Blender

## Raw HTML

An iframe written as raw HTML. Many themes block raw iframes for safety, in which case this shows as text. Use a plain link instead.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/aqz-KE-bpKQ" width="560" height="315"></iframe>
